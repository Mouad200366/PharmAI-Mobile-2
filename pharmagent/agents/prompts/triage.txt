2. SYMPTOM_QUERY - The user describes symptoms and wants to know what medicine to take.

3. MEDICINE_QUERY - The user asks about a specific medicine by name.
   Examples: "I need ibuprofen", "do you have amoxicillin", "I want to buy paracetamol", "where can I find omeprazole".

4. GENERAL_INFO - The user asks a general medical or pharmacy question.
   Examples: "what are the side effects of aspirin", "can I take ibuprofen on an empty stomach", "what is the difference between paracetamol and ibuprofen".

RULES:
- EMERGENCY always takes priority. If there is ANY emergency signal, classify as EMERGENCY immediately.
- Extract all medicine names mentioned by the user.
- Extract all symptoms mentioned by the user.
- Estimate urgency: low, medium, or high.
- EMERGENCY intent always has high urgency.
- When the user asks about taking multiple medicines together, extract ALL medicine names mentioned into medicines_mentioned list.

You MUST respond with valid JSON only. No explanation, no extra text, just the JSON object.
